Automated 7-Period background timetable comparison and instant substitute finder for CSE Department.

👨‍🏫

Faculty Quick Guide

1
Sign in:

Enter your Faculty ID or Name with your password. Each faculty member sees only their personal timetable.

2
Select Semester & Period:

Tap on any semester (1st Year to 6th Sem) and click the Period 1 to 7 slot you need to substitute.

3
Contact Available Teachers:

Free teachers appear below with 1-Click Call and WhatsApp.

4
Update & Edit Your Timetable:

In Update & Edit, upload a photo/PDF, auto-populate from the master timetable, or click any slot to edit and save.

👑

Admin Mode & Policy

1
Single Admin Lock:

Only ONE Admin is allowed institutional-wide. Enter your personal email to receive the 6-digit verification code.

2
Master Timetable Authority:

Admins can add/delete period slots in Update & Edit → 👑 Semester Master Timetable.

3
Manage Exam Schedules:

In Exam Invigilation, upload photos/PDFs of duty notices and delete past finished exams with 1-click.
